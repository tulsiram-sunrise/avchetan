# Avchetan User Guide

> Draft — describes planned behavior. Pages are completed as each feature ships.

Avchetan is a personal logger and planner. Type (or say) anything — a workout, a meal, a thought, a to-do — and Avchetan's AI organizes it for you. It works on your Windows PC and your Android phone, and **you can run it entirely for free on your own computer.**

## Start here

1. [Choose your setup](getting-started.md) — which of the three setups fits you.
2. Follow the setup guide for your choice:
   - [Just one device](setup-standalone.md)
   - [Home Hub — your PC + your phone, free and private](setup-home-hub.md)
   - [Cloud server — for advanced users](setup-cloud.md)
3. [Pair your phone](pair-your-phone.md)
4. [Choose AI models](ai-models.md)
5. [Capture on Windows](capture-on-windows.md): the shortcut, tags, and which key saves
6. [Use Avchetan on Android](capture-on-android.md): entries, tasks, and asking questions

## Downloads & source

- Installers (Windows) and the Android app (APK) are published on the project's **GitHub Releases** page.
- Avchetan is free and open source under the **AGPL-3.0** license. The source code is on GitHub.
