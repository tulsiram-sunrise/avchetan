# Avchetan User Guide

Avchetan is a personal logger and planner. Type (or say) anything — a workout, a meal, a thought, a to-do — and Avchetan's AI organizes it for you. It works on your Windows PC and your Android phone, and **you can run it entirely for free on your own computer.**

## Start here

1. [Choose your setup](getting-started.md) — which of the three setups fits you.
2. Follow the setup guide for your choice:
   - [Just one device](setup-standalone.md)
   - [Home Hub — your PC + your phone, free and private](setup-home-hub.md)
   - [Cloud server — for advanced users](setup-cloud.md)
3. [Pair your phone](pair-your-phone.md)
4. [Choose AI models](ai-models.md)
5. [Capture on Windows](capture-on-windows.md): the shortcut, tags, and which key saves
6. [Use Avchetan on Android](capture-on-android.md): entries, tasks, and asking questions
7. [Voice](voice.md): say an entry instead of typing it, offline, on the PC and the phone
8. [People and promises](people.md): who you saw, what you owe them and what they owe you
9. [Find an entry](capture-on-windows.md#find-an-entry): search your entries, tasks and people on the PC with Ctrl+F
10. [Tag and project pages](capture-on-windows.md#tag-and-project-pages): a tag's or project's entries, totals and open tasks, on the PC and [the phone](capture-on-android.md#tags-and-projects)
11. [Trends](capture-on-windows.md#trends): one of your numbers (distance, sleep, weight…) over 4 weeks, 3 months or a year, on the PC

## Downloads & source

- The Windows installer and the Android app (APK) are on the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/tulsiram-sunrise/avchetan/releases/latest). [Install, update and uninstall](install-and-update.md) explains each file, how to check a download, and how to update.
- Avchetan is free and open source under the **AGPL-3.0** license. The source code is on GitHub.
