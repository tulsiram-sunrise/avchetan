# Choose your setup

> Draft — describes planned behavior.

Answer two questions:

**1. Do you want your entries on more than one device (for example your PC and your phone)?**
- **No** → [Just one device](setup-standalone.md). The simplest setup; you can add more devices later without losing anything.
- **Yes** → go to question 2.

**2. Do you have a PC that is switched on most of the day, with a reasonably good graphics card?**
- **Yes** → [Home Hub](setup-home-hub.md). Your PC does all the work: it stores your data, runs the AI models (with the free Ollama app) and keeps your phone in sync. It costs nothing, and nothing leaves your home.
- **No, or I want it reachable everywhere without my PC** → [Cloud server](setup-cloud.md). You rent a small server. It needs some technical comfort and costs a few dollars a month, plus AI usage if you use paid AI services.

| | Just one device | Home Hub | Cloud |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Free | Hosting + AI usage |
| Phone + PC in sync | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Works without internet | ✓ | ✓ at home (or via Tailscale) | Needs internet to sync |
| Private | ✓ | ✓ (with local AI) | Depends on your choices |
| Difficulty | Easiest | Easy (guided) | Advanced |

You can switch setups later; your entries are kept.
