# People and promises

Avchetan notices the people you name in your entries and keeps track of what you promised them, and what they promised you. You don't have to do anything special: write as you normally do.

```
lunch with Priya, need to send her the photos by Friday
Farhan said he'll return my drill by Sunday
```

The first entry gives Priya a promise: *Send Priya the photos*, due Friday, under **You owe**. The second gives a task you're waiting on: *Get the drill back from Farhan*, due Sunday, under **Waiting on Farhan**.

A task done for or given to someone counts as a promise to them: *send*, *give*, *pay*, *return*, *reply*, *call back*. Meeting someone ("meet Arvind at 5") isn't a promise. Neither is a promise between two other people ("Kabir promised Lata he'd fix her bike").

## Where to find them

- **PC:** open Avchetan from the tray and choose **People** in the sidebar, under Tasks.
- **Phone:** **More → People**.

The list shows everyone your entries name. People with open promises come first, overdue ones on top, then the people you saw most recently. **Last seen** is the last day you were with them or talked to them. A day you only wrote *about* them doesn't count.

Tap or click a person to see:

- **You owe:** open tasks you promised them.
- **Waiting on *name*:** open tasks they promised you.
- **Done:** promises that are finished.
- **Entries:** every entry that mentions them, newest first. On the PC, click one to open that day. On the phone, tap it to open the entry.

Tick a promise to mark the task done, as on the Tasks page.

## Fix a promise

The AI sometimes gets a promise wrong. Each one has a fix:

- **Not a promise** removes the link and keeps the task. On the PC it's the **×** next to *Promise to …* on a task, or the button on the person's page. On the phone, open the task and tap **Not a promise**. Organizing the entry again won't bring the link back.
- **Add one by hand:**
  - **PC:** on any task (Tasks page or Days), click **Person…**. Choose **Promise to** or **Waiting on**, type a name, and press **Add**.
  - **Phone:** open a task and tap **Promise to…** or **Waiting on…**. Pick a person or add a new name.

## Tasks you're waiting on

A task you're waiting on someone for isn't your work, so:

- The **Tasks** page shows it in its own group, **Waiting on others**, after your own tasks.
- The **daily brief** leaves it out of the tasks that rolled over. Once it's due, the brief lists it separately, like *Waiting on Farhan: Get the drill back from Farhan (due Sun 8 Nov)*, with **Remind me tomorrow**, which moves its date one day later.

## The same person under two names

"Rahul" and "Rahul Sharma", or a misspelled "Pria", can show up as two people. Avchetan suggests likely pairs at the top of the People list (**Same person?**), but never joins them by itself.

- **Merge into …** moves everything to one person. Both names are kept, so new entries that use either name land there. Merging can't be undone.
- **No** hides that suggestion for good on this device.

On a person's page you can also:

- **Rename** them. On the PC, click the name. On the phone, tap **Rename or merge**. The old name is kept as another name for them.
- **Merge** them into anyone else. On the PC, use **Merge into…**. On the phone, use **Rename or merge**, then pick the person.

Renames and merges reach your other devices with sync.

## Ask about people

In Ask (`?` in the capture box on the PC, or the Ask tab on the phone):

- *What do I owe Rahul?*
- *What is Farhan supposed to send me?*
- *When did I last see Aman?*
- *Who did I talk to about the migration?*

Answers cite the entries they come from, as usual.

## Entries from before this version

Entries organized by an older version of Avchetan have people, but few promises. While some of them could hold promises, the People page shows **Look for promises in older entries**, with a count. It organizes those entries again in the background: older entries that have a task and name someone.

- It uses your AI once for each entry. With a cloud AI, its free-tier limits apply.
- Tasks you edited stay as they are.
- [Private entries](ai-models.md#private-entries) wait for a local AI, as always.

## Privacy

People and promises are worked out from your entries by the same AI that organizes them, and the [privacy rules](privacy.md) are the same. A person named only in private entries shows on your own People page with a 🔒. They are never named to a cloud AI, not in Ask and not when organizing other entries.
